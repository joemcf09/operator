# Real-world operator branding pack

This directory contains one offline PNG branding asset for each of the 103
operator catalog entries.

- 84 assets are normalized from a specific Wikimedia Commons file. The exact
  file page, source URL, license label, attribution field, artist, and credit
  supplied by Commons are recorded in `sources.json`.
- 19 entries use original FINAL MANIFEST fallback artwork because a credible current
  Commons logo was not available or the automated result identified another
  airline, an obsolete adjacent brand, or an aircraft photograph.

The deliberate fallback entries are Air Transport International, Cargojet
Airways, United Cargo, Sun Country Cargo, ASL Airlines Belgium, ASL Airlines
Ireland, Challenge Airlines Israel, Martinair Cargo, Saudia Cargo, Astral
Aviation, Hong Kong Air Cargo, China Airlines Cargo, China Cargo Airlines,
China Southern Cargo, China Eastern Cargo, China Postal Airlines, Suparna
Airlines, Garuda Indonesia Cargo, and SriLankan Cargo.

Real-world names and logos remain trademarks of their respective owners. Their
presence identifies a simulated operator and does not imply sponsorship or
endorsement. Wikimedia licensing metadata concerns copyright only; trademark
